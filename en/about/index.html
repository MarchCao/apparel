About HAICE Apparel

HAICE Apparel supports apparel OEM / ODM production in China for overseas clients.

Working with partner factories in China, we support specification confirmation, sample making, mass production, quality checks, and delivery management.

We communicate in Chinese, Japanese, English, and Korean.

HAICE Apparel is not a factory itself — we are the production support partner connecting you with partner factories in China. Manufacturing is handled by the partner factories; we handle communication and coordinate the entire production process.

What We Do

  • Client communication in Chinese, Japanese, English, and Korean
  • Requirement and specification confirmation
  • Quotation coordination
  • Sample arrangement and revision follow-up
  • Production progress follow-up
  • Quality and delivery communication
