Production Flow

Our standard flow, from inquiry to shipment.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Tell us about your product idea and requirements — via the form or email.

  2. 02

    Requirement Confirmation

    We confirm the details of your product.

    • Product
    • Material
    • Quantity
    • Size
    • Color
    • Specification
    • Target price
    • Delivery schedule
  3. 03

    Quotation

    Based on the confirmed details, we coordinate with the factory and present a quotation.

  4. 04

    Sample

    Once details are settled, we produce a sample.

  5. 05

    Sample Confirmation

    You review the sample and share any revision requests.

  6. 06

    Mass Production

    After your order is confirmed, we move into mass production.

  7. 07

    Quality Check

    We carry out production quality checks and coordinate with the factory as needed.

  8. 08

    Shipping

    We arrange shipment according to your order and destination.
