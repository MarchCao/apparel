FAQ

Answers to questions we hear often.

Do you support OEM?
Yes. We coordinate production based on your designs and specifications.
Do you support ODM?
Yes. The workable scope depends on the product and requirements — please talk to us first.
Can you make samples?
Yes. Sample production can be discussed according to the product.
What is the MOQ?
MOQ depends on product type, material, specifications, and production requirements. We confirm with the factory and let you know.
How long does production take?
Lead time depends on product specifications, quantity, materials, and the production schedule. We confirm based on your specific requirements.
Can you communicate in Japanese?
Yes. We communicate in Chinese, Japanese, English, and Korean.
Can you arrange shipping?
Shipping arrangements can be discussed according to the order and destination.
