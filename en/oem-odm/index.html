OEM / ODM Apparel Production

Production models tailored to your requirements.

OEM

What Is OEM?

You provide the designs, specifications, samples, or technical requirements, and we coordinate production accordingly — staying faithful to what you supply.

ODM

What Is ODM?

Based on your requirements, we support product development through production using our existing manufacturing capabilities. The workable scope is discussed and agreed with you in advance, depending on the product.

We never claim design or development capabilities beyond what we can actually deliver. We tell you exactly what is possible before we start.

What We Handle

Standing between you and the factory, we coordinate the entire process so production runs smoothly.

Requirement & specification confirmation

We organize product, material, quantity, size, color, specifications, target price, and delivery schedule — and align them with the factory.

Quotation coordination

We coordinate with the factory based on confirmed details and present a quotation.

Sample arrangement

We arrange sample production and follow up so revisions move quickly.

Mass production follow-up

We follow production schedule, materials, quality, and delivery throughout.

Quality & delivery communication

We communicate quality checks and delivery adjustments with the factory on your behalf.

Shipment coordination

We support shipment arrangements according to your order and destination.

Tell us about your project

Share your product category, target quantities, or timeline — whatever you have so far. We will respond in your language.
